Skill Gauntlet — Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Skill Gauntlet ("the App," "we," "us") is a single-player reflex and skill game. This page explains what information the App and the services it relies on collect, how it's used, and who it's shared with. It applies wherever the App is available (currently Android; other platforms may follow).

The short version: the App itself has no accounts, no servers, and doesn't collect anything about you. The exceptions are the two optional Google services it integrates with — Google Play Games Services (for leaderboards) and Google AdMob (for the optional "watch an ad to continue" feature) — and a time check the Daily Challenge and Weekly Gauntlet make with a public time server. All three are covered in detail below.

Information stored only on your device

The App keeps the following locally on your device, using Android's standard app-storage mechanism. None of it is ever transmitted to us or to any server we operate — we don't operate one:

  • Your highest level reached, and your best results in the Daily Challenge and Weekly Gauntlet
  • Your records: how many full runs you've completed in each mode, when you first cleared all 100 levels, and which Daily Challenge and Weekly Gauntlet you last fully cleared
  • Leaderboard scores waiting to be sent, if they couldn't be sent yet (for example, while offline), and your best score on each leaderboard
  • Your rewards calendar: how many days you've played, the last day counted, your saved revives, and which cosmetics you've chosen
  • Which Google Play Games account each set of progress belongs to (its player ID), so that each account you sign in with keeps its own progress
  • Sound and haptics on/off preferences

This data is removed automatically if you uninstall the App, or manually if you clear the App's storage from your device's system settings.

Google Play Games Services (Leaderboards)

If you choose to view or submit to leaderboards, the App uses Google Play Games Services, tied to your Google account. When you do, the App submits up to six scores, associated with your Google Play Games profile:

  • Your highest level ever reached
  • When you first cleared all 100 levels (the Hall of Fame)
  • When you fully cleared the current Daily Challenge, and the current Weekly Gauntlet
  • How many full runs you've completed, with the count for each mode
  • How many days you've played, and the date of the last day counted (not shown in the App; it lets a reinstall restore your rewards calendar without counting the same day twice)

Each score also carries your current badge from the rewards calendar, so other players see it next to your name on the leaderboards.

When you sign in, the App also reads your own scores back, so your records return after a reinstall. We never see or store this data ourselves; it's held and displayed entirely by Google Play Games Services, governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can manage or delete this data through your Google Account, and you can decline to sign in to Play Games entirely — the App works the same otherwise, just without leaderboards.

Time check

Some parts of the App depend on the real date and time, which must not depend on your phone's own clock: which Daily Challenge and Weekly Gauntlet are current, the time a leaderboard clear is stamped with (including clearing all 100 levels), which leaderboard entries are today's, and whether today counts as a new day on your rewards calendar. For these, the App asks a public time server (Google's time.google.com) for the current time, using the standard NTP protocol — when you open the App or return to it, when you open the Daily Challenge, the Weekly Gauntlet or the leaderboards, and when a clear needs its time stamped. The request carries no information about you or your device; like any internet request, it reaches the server from your IP address. This is why the Daily Challenge and Weekly Gauntlet need an internet connection. The main game works fully offline; a clear made offline is stamped the next time you're online.

Google AdMob (rewarded ads)

If you choose to watch a rewarded video ad to continue a run, the App uses Google AdMob to deliver it. This is the only place ads appear in the App — there are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt you uninvited. Showing an ad may involve Google and its advertising partners collecting device and advertising identifiers, approximate location, and ad-interaction data, for ad delivery, measurement, and (unless you've opted out) personalization. This collection is governed by Google's Ads Privacy Policy, not by us — we don't receive or store any of it.

You can limit ad personalization for your device through your device's own ad settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads), or opt out of personalized ads generally through Google's Ads Settings.

What we don't do

No account systemNothing to sign up for, no password, no email collected by the App itself.
No analytics/tracking SDKsBeyond the two Google services and the time check above, the App doesn't include any analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking libraries of our own.
No sensitive permissionsThe App never requests access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or location.
No data saleWe don't sell, rent, or trade any information, because we don't hold any to begin with.

Contacting us directly

The App's Settings screen has a "Contact Developer" option that opens your own email app, addressed to us, with nothing sent automatically — the App doesn't read or transmit anything from your device when you use it. If you choose to send that email, we receive whatever you write and your email address, the same as any email you send anyone.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information (for example, via the contact email above), please reach out at the address below and we'll delete it.
